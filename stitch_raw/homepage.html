Common Questions

What are the school timings? add_circle
Our preschool programs run from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Daycare facilities are available until 6:30 PM.
Do you provide transport facilities? add_circle
Yes, we have a fleet of GPS-enabled buses with female attendants for safe commuting.
What is the teacher-student ratio? add_circle
We maintain a strict 1:10 ratio for Nursery and 1:8 for Playgroup to ensure individual attention.
